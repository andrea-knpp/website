Welcome! I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Faculty of Economics and Social Sciences at the University of Potsdam. I received my Ph.D. in Political Science from the University of Bologna in 2026.

My research focuses on international organizations (particularly the United Nations and NATO), the politics of multilateral diplomacy, and conflict resolution through foreign interventions. I mainly use quantitative methods, including social network analysis, text-as-data approaches, and survey experiments, but I also have experience conducting expert interviews.

Beyond academia, I worked at the "Enablement and Resilience Section - Defence Policy and Planning Unit" (DPP-ERS) at NATO Headquarters in 2023 and was a research consultant for the NATO Stability Policing Centre of Excellence (SP CoE) in 2024–2025.
