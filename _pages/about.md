Welcome! I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Faculty of Economics and Social Sciences of the University of Potsdam. I earned my Ph.D. in Political Science from the University of Bologna in 2026.

My research focuses on International Organizations (particularly the United Nations and NATO), multilateral diplomacy and conflict resolution. I mainly use quantitative methods like social network analysis, text-as-data approaches, and survey experiments, but I also have experience conducting expert interviews.

Beyond academia, I worked at the "Enablement and Resilience Section - Defence Policy and Planning Unit" (DPP-ERS) at NATO Headquarters in 2023 and was a research consultant for the NATO Stability Policing Centre of Excellence (SP CoE) in 2024–2025.
