### United Nations Security Council Resolution Authors (UNSCRA) dataset

The United Nations Security Council Resolution Authors (UNSCRA) dataset provides information on 2,179 draft resolutions submitted at the United Nations Security Council. It tracks both sponsors (1990-2023) and lead negotiators (2008-2023).
