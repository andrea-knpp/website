Welcome! I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Faculty of Economics and Social Sciences at the University of Potsdam. I received my Ph.D. in Political Science from the University of Bologna in 2026.

My research focuses on International Organizations (particularly the United Nations and NATO), the politics on multilateral diplomacy and conflict resolution through foreign interventions. I mainly work with quantitative methods like social network analysis, text-as-data and survey esperiments, but I also have experience with expert interviews.

I worked at the “Enablement and Resilience Section - Defence Policy and Planning Unit” (DPP-ERS) at NATO headquarters in 2023 and was a research consultant for the NATO Stability Policing Center of Excellence (SP CoE) in 2024-2025. I held visiting researcher positions at Uppsala University and Columbia University.
